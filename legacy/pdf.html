PARAM MINHAS

THE PROBLEM

THE SIX-PAIR LIE.

The global sports industry sells a shoe rack — one for running, one for the gym, one for the court, one for the trail, one for the weekend, one for the wedding.

That model was invented in a market with garages, closets and disposable income. It has never once described how anybody in this country lives.

We're not fighting Nike. We're fighting the idea that owning one pair means you're not serious.

"The most admired object in an Indian house is never the newest one."
